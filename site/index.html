03Process / Proceso

Eight to fourteen weeks, one conversation.

A single Slack channel. Weekly show-and-tells. No phases, no sign-off dramas — just four weeks that look like brand, then four that look like product, then launch.

◐ Phase 01 W1–2

Listen & Frame

A week of interviews. We surface the thing you actually sell, and the story nobody's told yet. You leave with a positioning doc we'd defend in court.

◑ Phase 02 W3–5

Identity

Logo, type, color, motion, voice. Delivered as a working guidelines site at brand.yourname.com — not a PDF that'll get lost.

◒ Phase 03 W5–11

Product & Site

We design in Figma and build in Next.js the same week. Every Monday is a live walkthrough on a staging URL you can click.

◓ Phase 04 W12–14

Launch & Hand-off

Ship day, press kit, a walkthrough of the repo. Thirty days of free tweaks. Then, if you want, we stay on retainer.

04Studio / Estudio

We're a studio of five in Monterrey. We chose to stay small so every project gets the founders, not the B-team.

Manifesto · 01

We think the split between brand and product is a nineteenth-century accident. A launch is one thing. Typography is UX. A button is a piece of copy. We hire people who don't need a handoff diagram to believe this.

Every engagement starts with one question: what does the first user see? — and works backwards from there.

Manifesto · 02

We take three clients at a time. That's not a humblebrag — that's the math for keeping founders and engineers in the same room for fourteen weeks without anyone quietly quitting.

If we say yes to you, we're saying no to eleven other companies that quarter. We take that seriously. You should too.
